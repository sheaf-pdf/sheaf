# M11 proposal 16: Signatures in a redacted or sanitized copy are removed,

Part of [M11: Security & redaction](../README.md) · [all proposals](README.md)

**Signatures in a redacted or sanitized copy are removed,** and the
report says so. They would be invalid anyway, since the copy is a
full rewrite. M12 then deals with removal and signing in the binder.
