# B62: Packing on Append

Status: open

- **Found:** 2026-10-26, object streams slice 2 (decision 8)
- **Where:** `crates/sheaf-doc/src/serialize/append.rs` (an update's
  section) and `serialize/xref.rs` (`Section::stream` deflates only a
  full write's rows); `crates/sheaf-doc/src/materialize/append.rs`
- **Severity:** none: a feature, logged under the B number it was given
- **What happens:** an Append writes each object it changed on its own,
  in the base's cross-reference kind, and an update's cross-reference
  stream uncompressed, even onto a file that packs its objects in
  object streams. A Rewrite packs them again and deflates its stream
  (object streams slice 2); an update doesn't, by decision 8: its few
  objects gain little, and a plain update is what signature validators
  read most easily.
- **Expected:** a large update onto a file that used object streams (OCR
  text or a watermark on every page of a long file) packs what may be
  packed into object streams of its own, numbered past `/Size`, and
  deflates its cross-reference stream under the PNG Up predictor, as a
  full write does; a small one, and any onto a signed file, may stay as
  it is.
- **Repro:** open a file whose cross-reference section is a stream, add
  a mark and save (an Append): the section's objects are on their own,
  and its cross-reference stream has no `/Filter`.
- **Notes:**
  - **Signature validators:** an update may hold object streams and a
    compressed cross-reference stream (ISO 32000-1 7.5.8), but check what
    Acrobat, pyHanko and PDFium-based validators make of one before
    packing onto a signed file: DocMDP and "changes after signing"
    checks diff the objects an update adds.
  - **What exists:** `objstm::packable`, `objstm::container`,
    `group::group`, `graph::page_uses`, `Entry::Packed` and the
    compressed stream are all in `serialize/` and `materialize/` now;
    an update would need `Section::append` to compress, and its
    containers numbered as an Append numbers anything new.
  - **Overlap:** M14's Optimize packs every file whatever its source
    (decision 1): packing an update is a separate choice, there or
    here. See `plan/complete/object-streams.md`, decision 8 and slice
    2's notes.
