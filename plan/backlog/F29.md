# F29: Research: how the Arlington tier holds a damaged form pdf-lint reads nothing of

Status: open

- **Found:** 2026-10-26, Claude, during the pdf-lint item's slice 1
  (the corpus tier on pdf-lint)
- **What:** decide, on merit, how `just tier-arlington` checks a save of
  a damaged form whose `startxref` names no cross-reference. sheaf opens
  such a form through lopdf's recovery and saves it; pdf-lint's bytes
  reader reads what's written, so it reaches none of the form's objects
  (4422502: nothing from the trailer) or few (1819139: the pages and
  fields are unresolved). A fault the form has in an object sheaf merges
  then isn't carried: 1819139's fields' `/DR`, deprecated from PDF 1.5
  (78 findings in its two Rewrites), and 4422502's pages' `/Tabs`, too
  new for its PDF 1.4 (9 in its two Rewrites).
- **Why:** sheaf's old checker read the form through the same lopdf
  `Source` as the save, so the two read alike; pdf-lint's reader is the
  one that checks a writer's syntax (the item's decision 2). Each answer
  changes what the tier holds a damaged form to.
- **Overlap:** the [pdf-lint item](../complete/pdf-lint.md); B46 (which
  object a merged finding is carried from).
- **Notes:** held by two rows of
  `crates/sheaf-ops/tests/arlington_allowed.tsv`, scoped to the two
  forms, with the counts above. Options: check such a form with
  `pdf_lint::Qpdf::read` (qpdf's reconstruction, which reads 4422502
  and fails on 1819139); hold only what sheaf built in its saves; or
  keep the rows. Not chasing either reader's recovery: broken files
  aren't targets.
