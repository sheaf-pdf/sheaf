# B65: Under X11 a new window sometimes opens 20 px larger each way

Status: open

- **Found:** 2026-10-27, Claude, during the gpui-pre 0.3.10 bump
- **Where:** sheaf-app's window under X11 (Xwayland on GNOME):
  `open_workspace` in `crates/sheaf-app/src/main.rs` asks for 1200×850,
  and `sheaf_ui::frame` sets the client inset (`crates/sheaf-ui/src/frame.rs`)
- **Severity:** annoyance
- **What happens:** over six launches each with a fresh `SHEAF_CONFIG_DIR`
  (no saved bounds), the X window was 1200×850 in five of gpui-pre 0.3.9's
  and four of 0.3.10's, and 1220×870 in the rest: 10 px more on every
  side, the width of the shadow inset. The larger window fits the page at
  89% instead of 87%.
- **Expected:** the same size on every launch.
- **Repro:** launch `sheaf` several times under Xwayland
  (`env -u WAYLAND_DISPLAY DISPLAY=:0`), each with a new
  `SHEAF_CONFIG_DIR`, and read the window's size with `xwininfo`.
- **Notes:** the same in both GPUI versions, and neither GPUI's X11 backend
  nor its inset code changed between them. Likely a race between mapping
  the window and setting `_GTK_FRAME_EXTENTS` (the client inset), which
  mutter reads to tell the shadow from the window. Not checked: Wayland
  (no window grab there), and whether saved bounds then restore larger.
