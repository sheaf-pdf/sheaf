# B66: On Wayland, first pixels sometimes take 3–4 s instead of 0.3 s

Status: open

- **Found:** 2026-10-27, Claude, during the gpui-pre 0.3.10 bump
- **Where:** sheaf-app's startup under Wayland (GNOME), as `--timing`'s
  "first pixels ready" reports it
- **Severity:** annoyance
- **What happens:** ten alternating launches of `forms/irs/f1040es.pdf`
  (five on gpui-pre 0.3.9, five on 0.3.10, with `--timing --selftest-save`
  and a `--script` that quits): "ready" came 0.28–0.41 s after launch each
  time, and first pixels 0.29–0.43 s after launch in five runs but
  3.15–3.83 s in the other five (two of 0.3.9's, three of 0.3.10's). The open has finished by then; only the
  first frame waits.
- **Expected:** first pixels right after "ready" on every launch
  ([the gpui-kit spike](../../docs/gpui-kit-eval.md) measured 283–317 ms).
- **Repro:** launch it about ten times from a terminal on GNOME Wayland
  with `--timing` and a script ending in `{"quit": true}`; compare the two
  lines.
- **Notes:** the same in both GPUI versions, so not the bump. A wait of
  several seconds looks like the compositor holding frame callbacks. Check
  first whether it happens only when the window opens unfocused or behind
  another window (these runs started from a terminal while other windows
  were in use); if so, it is the harness's. [B60](B60.md) is Windows'
  launch time.
