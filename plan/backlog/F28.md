# F28: Register the `SHEF` prefix before the first release

Status: open

- **Found:** 2026-10-25, Greg, when sheaf's private names took the
  prefix (F24)
- **What:** register `SHEF` as sheaf's developer prefix in the ISO PDF
  names registry Adobe keeps
  ([adobe/pdf-names-list](https://github.com/adobe/pdf-names-list)): an
  issue from its template, one per prefix, giving the prefix, an
  organization (name, postal address, website) and a contact (name,
  title, email). Adobe checks it against every registration, its private
  pre-2008 ones too, adds it to the public list and emails a
  confirmation.
- **Why:** ISO 32000-2 Annex E has second-class names begin with a
  registered prefix, and sheaf writes five (`private::KEYS`). Until it's
  registered, another developer could take `SHEF`. Greg (2026-10-25):
  hold off until there's a release.
- **Overlap:** the release work (`plan/topics/installer.md`,
  `plan/topics/code-signing.md`)
- **Notes:**
  - `SHEF` wasn't in the public list on 2026-10-25 (106 prefixes, none
    starting "SH"); the issue is the real check. If it's taken, the names
    are renamed again, and `private.rs`'s test that every name sheaf
    writes starts `SHEF_` says where.
  - It's free; the last eight registrations closed in 1–14 days.
  - Everything in the issue is public, the address included, and the
    registry's CSV publishes the prefix, the contact's name and email,
    the organization and the date: a project email is the safer choice.
