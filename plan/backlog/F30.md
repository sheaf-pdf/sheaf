# F30: Upstream: pdf-structure XRef stream cap check before inflate and `/XRefStm` deduplication

Status: open

- **Found:** 2026-11-18, M18 slice 8 (`sheaf` reads through `pdf-structure`)
- **What:** in `sheaf-pdf/pdf-lint`'s `pdf-structure`:
  1. Check a cross-reference stream dictionary's `/Size` and `/Index`
     against `Limits::entries` before `decode_stream` inflates its rows
     (`read.rs`), so a 50M-entry `/Size` bomb is refused from its
     dictionary without inflating `rows_size` bytes.
  2. Deduplicate hybrid `/XRefStm` streams across `/Prev` sections by
     where their indirect object header ends, so 1,000 sections pointing
     `/XRefStm` at one stream decode it once.
- **Why:** `sheaf_doc::pdf::reader` currently runs a lightweight header
  pre-check (`precheck_xref_stream_caps` + `BlankedSource`) ahead of
  `Reading::open` to enforce `a_cross_reference_stream_bomb_is_refused_from_its_dictionary`
  and `a_cross_reference_stream_many_sections_share_is_read_once` in
  milliseconds; landing both in `pdf-structure` lets that pre-check go.
- **Overlap:** M18 slice 8.
- **Notes:** small change in `crates/pdf-structure/src/read.rs`.
